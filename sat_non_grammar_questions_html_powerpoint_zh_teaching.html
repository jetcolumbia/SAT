第 1 章 · Transitions

为什么Transitions重要

  • Transition questions 是Writing部分最常见的题型之一。
  • 每套test大约会出现8到10道transition items。
  • 大概占全套题的15-20%。
  • 所以学生需要熟悉常见和不那么常见的transitional words and phrases。

SAT Transition题到底考什么

Core skill

  • 判断某个transition是否真的在两个句子，或一个句子的两个部分之间，建立了logical connection。
  • 通常四个answer choices在grammar上都没有问题。
  • 真正考的是：在context里哪个选项最符合logic。

第一步：先判断句子关系

看选项前先做这件事

  • 空格在一个transition的位置。
  • 所以正确的word必须说明：带空格的句子和前一句/前一段信息是什么关系。
  • 先往前看，判断这个句子和前文的logical relationship，再去看choices。

Eliot例题 · 先看logic，再看choices

Relationship

  • 第一句：The Waste Land出版后只卖出约330本。
  • 第二句：Eliot被迫寻找其他收入来源。
  • 第二个statement是第一个statement的result。
  • 所以正确选项必须表达result。

Eliot例题 · 完整题目

Although T.S. Eliot devoted several years to writing "The Waste Land," it sold only about 330 copies in the six months following its publication in 1922. __ Eliot was forced to seek other sources of income. In the mid-1920s, he took a job as the director of a new publishing firm called Faber & Faber.

Teacher's note（讲解）

Consequently 明确表示第二句话是第一句话的result / consequence。

Eliot例题 · Process of Elimination

用每个transition的function来排除

  • Alternatively 表示contrast，所以不符合。
  • In fact 用来强调前一句，不符合。
  • Moreover 表示“in addition / furthermore”，通常引入新的supporting idea，也不符合。
  • 剩下的就是 Consequently。

Key Takeaway：Function Beats Sound

为什么这个方法更快

  • 不要靠“读起来顺不顺”；要靠transition的purpose / function排除。
  • 如果你先知道正确word应该承担什么function，就不需要把每个选项都塞回原文试读。
  • 这样不容易被“听起来好像也可以”的选项干扰。
  • 尤其能避开那些vaguely plausible但not-quite-right的答案。

Transition的三大类型

三大类型

  • Continuers: 两个句子表达similar ideas。
  • Cause-and-effect words: 一个动作/事实是另一个的result。
  • Contradictors: 两个句子表达different ideas。

Transition Type 1 · Continuers

Continuers

Continuers 表示两个句子在表达相似、延续或补充的ideas。

Correct: Erenna sirena, a relative of the jellyfish, is one of the rare marine organisms to emit red light. Indeed, only a few other deep-sea creatures produce a glow that colour.

Transition Type 2 · Cause-and-Effect

Cause-and-effect words

这类词表示一个action / fact 是另一个action / fact 的result。

Correct: The light from most quasars was emitted when the universe was only a fraction of its present age. Therefore, quasars offer an important clue to the appearance of the early universe.

Transition Type 3 · Contradictors

Contradictors

Contradictors 表示两个句子在表达不同、相反或转折的ideas。

Correct: An increased reliance on computerised systems can leave users vulnerable to cyberattacks. However, current defences are stronger than many people realise.

Transition题的解题流程

General rule

看选项之前，尽量先判断两个statements之间的precise relationship。

  • 重读两个句子，或一个句子的两个部分。
  • 判断它们之间的relationship，并尝试预测答案方向。
  • 再看answers，选择和这个relationship匹配的选项。

先预测Type，再找Word

如果看到...

  • Contrast: however, nevertheless, still, in contrast
  • Add similar idea: furthermore, moreover, in addition, similarly, likewise
  • Result: therefore, consequently, accordingly

另外留意...

  • Example: for example, for instance, specifically
  • Emphasis: indeed, in fact
  • 如果不能判断精确relationship，至少先判断大类：Continue / Cause-and-Effect / Contrast。

Elimination Rules + Shortcut

按relationship排除

  • 如果两个句子表达similar ideas，排除 but / however 这类contradictors。
  • 如果两个句子表达different ideas，排除continuers和cause-and-effect words，例如 for example 或 therefore。

Shortcut

如果两个或更多选项是grammar上等价的synonyms，可以同时排除，因为一道题不可能有两个正确答案。

例如：一个选项是 consequently，另一个是 therefore，通常可以都排除。

Antarctica例题 · Answer Logic

Opposing ideas

  • Sentence 1: Antarctica interior里生命难以thrive。
  • Sentence 2: Antarctic Peninsula和周边岛屿上生命可以thrive。
  • 这两句是opposing ideas，所以需要一个contradictor。
  • 因此 In contrast 是唯一可能的答案。

Rare Fine Distinction · 同一类Transition内部的细分

Important caveat

极少数情况下，College Board会要求学生在同一类transition内部做非常细的区分。

这种题只在一套non-administered exam中出现过一次，但知道这个点更稳。

Fine Distinction Example

Conditions in the interior of Antarctica are inhospitable to many forms of life: sub-zero temperatures, high winds, and extreme dryness make it impossible for most animals to survive. __ the Antarctic Peninsula and the surrounding islands have relatively mild temperatures and liquid water, allowing many species to thrive there.

Teacher's note（讲解）

虽然 still 和 however 很多时候可以互换，但这里 however 更强。

Still 有“despite this”的意思；但Antarctic Peninsula气候较温和，并不是“despite”内陆气候严酷而存在。它只是另一个location，有不同climate。

Harder Example · 不要被科学词汇带跑

抓essential information

  • Sentence 1: 少数species虽然面对Antarctica的harsh climate，仍然能够生活在那里。
  • Sentence 2: 列举了具体能生活在那里的species。
  • 正确transition必须是一个continuer，提示Sentence 2在给Sentence 1举examples。
  • 唯一匹配的是 Specifically。

Transitions in the Middle or End of a Sentence

看似只考一句，实际可能考两句

Transitions可以出现在一句话中间，被两个commas夹住。

这种结构看起来像在考同一句内部，但通常真正考的是：含transition的句子与前一句之间的relationship。

同一个意思，不同Placement

Version #1

Therefore, tropical marine mammals have very few fat reserves in their bodies.

Version #2

Tropical marine mammals, therefore, have very few fat reserves in their bodies.

Teacher's note（讲解）

差别只是style；两句meaning完全一样。两种写法里，transition都在把当前句子连接到前一个statement。

Middle-of-Sentence Transition题

Healthy arctic marine mammals have a thick layer of fat beneath the skin. Tropical marine mammals, __ have very few fat reserves in their bodies. As a result, many of them dwell primarily in the warm waters that surround coral reefs.

为什么它不是连接句子内部两半

Cross it out

把transition划掉，再把句子拆开看：

  1. Tropical marine mammals
  2. have very few fat reserves in their bodies

这两个fragment单独读时没有logical relationship。

它真正连接的是什么

它真正clarify的是当前句子和前一句之间的relationship。

Tropical Mammals · Answer Logic

需要Contrast

  • Previous sentence: arctic marine mammals有一层厚厚的fat。
  • Current sentence: tropical marine mammals几乎没有fat reserves。
  • 这两个ideas形成contrast: thick layer of fat vs. very few fat reserves。
  • However 是contradictor，所以正确。

Contradictor的Placement

1) Healthy arctic marine mammals have a thick layer of fat beneath the skin. However, tropical marine mammals have very few fat reserves in their bodies.

2) Healthy arctic marine mammals have a thick layer of fat beneath the skin. Tropical marine mammals, however, have very few fat reserves in their bodies.

3) Healthy arctic marine mammals have a thick layer of fat beneath the skin. Tropical marine mammals have very few fat reserves in their bodies, however.

Same function

三句话meaning相同：however都表示第二句opposes第一句。

Healthy arctic marine mammals have a thick layer of fat beneath their skin, allowing them to dwell in the frigid waters surrounding the Earth’s poles. Tropical marine mammals have few fat reserves in their bodies, __. As a result, they are largely confined to the warm waters that surround coral reefs.

End-of-Sentence题 · Key Point

Placement可能误导你

  • 即使transition在位置上紧挨着后一句，它考的仍然是“所在句”和“前一句”的relationship。
  • 这两句呈现contrasting ideas。
  • 所以 however 正确。

什么时候Transition真的连接句子两半

One-comma construction

有时transition在句子中间，确实是在连接同一句内部的两个部分。

这类transition通常只跟一个comma。

One-Comma Question

Tropical marine mammals have few fat reserves in their bodies and dwell primarily in the warm waters that surround coral reefs. In contrast, healthy arctic marine mammals have a thick layer of fat beneath their skin, __ they are able to tolerate much colder waters.

▣ Mark for Review

Which choice completes the text with the most logical transition?

A

so

B

for

C

yet

D

whereas

One-Comma Question · Answer Logic

Cross out the transition

  1. Healthy arctic marine mammals have a thick layer of fat beneath the skin.
  2. They are able to tolerate cold water.

第二半句是第一半句的result，所以需要cause-and-effect word。

为什么“so”胜出

For 可以表示“because”，但它会把logic反过来。Arctic mammals不是因为能忍受冷水所以有fat；而是因为有fat所以能忍受冷水。只有 so 建立正确relationship。

Glossary: Accordingly / Consequently

Accordingly / Consequently = therefore, as a result

Correct: Dolphins are highly social creatures. Consequently, they live in pods of up to a dozen animals.
Correct: Dolphins are highly social creatures. Accordingly, they live in pods of up to a dozen animals.

Glossary: Furthermore / Moreover

Furthermore / Moreover = in addition

Correct: Dolphins are social animals. Furthermore, they are highly intelligent.
Correct: Dolphins are social animals. Moreover, they are highly intelligent.

Glossary: In Fact / Indeed

用于emphasise前一句statement

Correct: Dolphins are highly intelligent. In fact, they are one of the smartest mammals.
Correct: Dolphins are highly intelligent. Indeed, they are one of the smartest mammals.

Glossary: Even So / Nevertheless / Still

Despite this, however

Correct: Dolphins are descended from land-dwelling animals. Even so, they can only survive in water.
Correct: Dolphins are descended from land-dwelling animals. Nevertheless, they can only survive in water.
Correct: Dolphins are descended from land-dwelling animals. Still, they can only survive in water.

Glossary: Whereas

Whereas = although

Correct: A salmon is a type of fish, whereas a dolphin is a type of mammal.

Glossary: As Such

As such = as a + noun

As such 是比较tricky的transition之一。

Correct: Dolphins are social animals. Because they are social animals, they live in pods of up to a dozen animals.
Also correct: Dolphins are social animals. As social animals, they live in pods of up to a dozen animals.
Smoother: Dolphins are social animals. As such, they live in pods of up to a dozen animals.

Glossary: Likewise / Meanwhile

Likewise = similarly, in the same way

Correct: As mammals, dolphins are warm-blooded. Likewise, they nourish their young with milk.

Meanwhile

字面意思是“at the same time”；但也常被用成 however，表示contrast。

Correct: Many people think of dolphins as fish. Meanwhile, they ignore scientific research, which long ago established that dolphins are actually mammals.

Glossary: Subsequently / That Is

Subsequently = afterward

Correct: In the 1980s, dolphin populations began to decline because too many animals were caught in fishing nets. Subsequently, fishing companies began taking steps to reassure customers that products were “dolphin safe.”

That is = in other words

引入definition或explanation，常跟在dash后面。

Correct: Dolphins are mammals - that is, they are warm-blooded and nourish their young with milk.

第 2 章 · Specific Focus (“Student Notes”)

Note-taking questions长什么样

  • 这是专门为digital SAT设计的题型。
  • 通常会给3-5条bullet points，内容是某个topic的research notes。
  • 问题会要求你找出：哪个statement最有效地使用notes中的信息，去introduce或emphasise某个particular idea。

Student Notes · Big Strategy

不要被吓到

  • 这些题看起来信息很多，所以容易显得复杂。
  • 但真正相关的信息通常只是一小部分。
  • 答案几乎总是藏在question本身里。
  • 正确选项只是把题目给出的specific focus重新表达出来。

Student Notes · Specific Focus

先找student wants to do什么

  • 每道note-taking question都会给出一个specific focus：student想present, introduce, specify, 或emphasise什么。
  • 这个instruction后面的信息，就是key word or phrase。
  • 检查answers时，只找和这个focus匹配的选项。
  • 只有一个选项会fit；找到就可以结束。

Student Notes · Important Reassurance

没有隐藏陷阱

  • 所有answer choices都会提到notes里真实出现的信息。
  • 你不用担心某个答案focus对了，但left-hand column里没提到。
  • SAT这类题的重点不是这种trickiness。

Focus Type 1 · Comparisons and Contrasts

Similar or different?

这类题要求你找到一个statement，说明两个人/事物是similar还是different。

根据题目emphasis，正确答案常会包含 like, unlike, 或 whereas 这类词。

Focus Type 2 · Familiar vs. Unfamiliar

Audience matters

这类题要求你把一个person, artwork, event等介绍给有背景知识或没有背景知识的audience。

  • 面向familiar audience的正确答案通常不包含基本背景信息。
  • 面向unfamiliar audience的正确答案需要包含基本background或historical information，而且可能比其他选项更长。

Birds’ bones are hollow, a trait that was shared by many dinosaur species.

D

Birds are descended from the theropod group of dinosaurs, which contained Tyrannosaurus rex.

Similarity Example · Matching Process

先扫similarity language

  • 题目要求说明modern birds和dinosaurs在某方面alike。
  • 任何表示difference的选项，比如含 although 或 whereas，都不对。
  • Choice A里的 however 表示contrast，所以A错。
  • Choice C里的 shared 表示similarity，所以C匹配focus。
  • Choice D提到birds descended from dinosaurs，但没有真正说明二者的similarity。

  • Her 2021 installation in Chicago featured photos, video, text, and furniture.

▣ Mark for Review

The student wants to emphasise the variety of materials employed by Weems to an audience unfamiliar with her work. Which choice most effectively uses relevant information from the notes to achieve this goal?

B

In 2021, Weems staged an installation in Chicago in which visitors were encouraged to participate directly in a recreation of a historic classroom.

Weems例题 · 正确答案必须做到什么

Key requirements（关键要求）

  • 必须emphasise Weems使用materials的variety。
  • 因为audience unfamiliar with Weems’s work，所以必须提供basic background，说明她是谁。
  • 因此正确答案很可能更长。

为什么C匹配

Choice C包含background information，并且用“a diverse set of media”以及photos, furniture, shadow puppets等examples，直接对应variety这个idea。

Weems例题 · 为什么其他选项不匹配

Elimination

  • A给了awards方面的context，但完全没有说materials。
  • B只谈一个installation，而且缺少关于Weems的background information。
  • D提到different materials，但没有把Weems介绍给unfamiliar audience。
  • 因此答案是C。
